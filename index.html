How to serve

  1. Read the order on the TV and grab the items.
  2. Cook patties on the grill, fill cups at the fountain. Take them when READY, not burnt or overfilled.
  3. Fryer: print Fries at the Fry Printer (right wall), or open the Freezer next to the fryers for McNuggets / a Chicken Patty. Throw it into the fryer with Q, pull it out with E when golden. Fries then need a mash of E to salt.
  4. Put everything on the tray, then press E on it empty-handed to serve.
  5. Fast, accurate, well-made orders earn tips and a higher ★ rating.

Burgers & supplies

  • Toast two buns in the Toaster, grill a patty, mash E at the Cutting Board to chop veggies.
  • Stack Bun → Patty → Veggies → Bun on the Assembly plate.
  • Watch the STOCK list (top right) and restock at the Supply Computer on the right wall.
  • Unwanted food goes in the Incinerator. Don't throw things at customers!

Specials

  • McDouble: Bun → Patty → Veggies → Patty → Veggies → Bun on the Assembly plate.
  • McCrispy: Bun → fried Chicken Patty → Veggies → Bun on the Assembly plate.
  • McFlurry (left wall): grab Ice Cream Mix, cool it in the Deep Freezer until READY, mash E at the Oreo Crusher, then add both to the McFlurry Mixer and mash E to mix.
  • Don't leave ice cream in too long: it freezes solid!
